# Satire vs. Facts

WASATCH BACK is satire. It should be fun for everyone and must never be mistaken for reporting.

## Fictional (everything in-game unless listed below)
- **MONOPEAK HOLDINGS**, **Chip Lodgeworth III**, **GONDOLUX™**, **GRANITECORE DATA**, every headline, sign, quote, price, date and gondola "ETA".
- The 5 development phases are an exaggerated, invented progression, not a prediction.
- No real company, official or person is depicted or quoted. Don't add real names to in-game content.

## Real-world context (About screen only)
The About screen gives a short, neutral summary with a pointer to official sources:

> In 2023 the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) issued a Record of Decision naming a gondola as its preferred long-term alternative for Little Cottonwood Canyon, with a phased approach beginning with improved bus service and tolling. The project has drawn both support and significant public opposition, and its funding and timeline remain subject to future decisions.

**Before any public release, re-verify this paragraph** against UDOT's Little Cottonwood Canyon project materials and current local reporting. Funding, legal and legislative status may have changed. Keep the in-game text short, sourced and free of opinion. The jokes live in the game; the facts stay factual.

## Guidelines for new content
1. Punch at systems and absurdity (paywalls, perpetual delays, PR-speak), not at individuals.
2. Invented names should be obviously invented. Check new brand names aren't real companies.
3. Never present a joke as a statistic.
4. Keep the "A work of satire" footer on the title screen and in store listings.
